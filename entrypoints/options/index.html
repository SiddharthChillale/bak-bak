Guide

Everything you need to get going, plus tips for getting the most out of your credits.

1. Get a Sarvam API key

Bak-Bak speaks with Sarvam AI's voices, so it needs your own Sarvam API key. It's free to sign up, and new accounts get free credits.

  1. Go to dashboard.sarvam.ai and sign in or create an account.
  2. Open the API keys section of the dashboard and create a new key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it into Setup above. A green Connected badge means it worked.

You pay Sarvam directly for what you listen to; Bak-Bak itself is free. Your key never leaves this browser except in requests to Sarvam.

2. Start listening

  • Open an article and click the Bak-Bak button in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+R.
  • Right-click the page and choose Read this page aloud.
  • To read just part of a page, select the text, right-click and choose Read aloud from here.

3. While it's reading

  • Jump to in the player lets you click any line to start reading from there (Esc cancels).
  • Collapse the player to a small pill, or drag it to any corner of the window.
  • Switch to Podcast for two hosts talking the article through, or Summary to hear just the gist. Nothing is generated until you press the button.

Tips for saving credits

  • Every sentence you hear is cached on this device, so going back or replaying is free.
  • Skip code blocks, tables, link previews and captions under Reading to send less text.
  • Long articles ask before starting; set the limit with Ask before reading longer than.
  • Usage & cache shows what you've listened to this month and an estimated cost.

If something isn't working

  • "Couldn't find an article." Some pages aren't laid out like articles. Select the text you want and choose Read aloud from here.
  • Nothing happens on a page. Browsers don't let extensions run on built-in pages (settings, new tab) or extension stores. Try a regular website.
  • Key errors or no audio. Check that the key is pasted in full and that your Sarvam account has credits left.
  • A shortcut doesn't work. Another extension may use it. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (or your browser's equivalent).
  • Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub.
