Read Aloud Settings

Sarvam

Stored in this browser's local extension storage only. Get one at dashboard.sarvam.ai.

Reading

Off by default: the extension only runs on a page when you click its button, use its menu or a shortcut. Turning this on asks your browser for access to all sites so it can mark readable pages with a dot on the toolbar icon and let you Alt+click a sentence before opening the player. Page text is still only sent to Sarvam when you press play.

Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+R read / play-pause · Alt+Shift+. next sentence · Alt+Shift+, previous sentence. Alt+click any sentence on the page to read from there. Change shortcuts in your browser's extension shortcut settings.

Podcast

Turns an article into a conversation between two hosts: Sarvam-105B writes the script (about ₹0.25 per article), then the hosts' voices read it (₹30 per 10,000 characters, like normal reading). Switch the player to Podcast (or right-click the page → “Make a podcast from this page…”), pick the two hosts there, press Write script, then Generate audio. Nothing is generated until you press those buttons.

These are the same host choices as in the player, saved per model. Scripts are cached, so reopening a podcast doesn't rewrite it.

Usage & cache

Estimated at ₹30 per 10,000 characters (Bulbul v3 list price). Cached sentences are not billed again.
